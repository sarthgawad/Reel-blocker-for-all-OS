How to Install in Your Browser

Load ReelShield into Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Opera in under 30 seconds.

1

Open Extensions Manager

In Chrome or Brave, type chrome://extensions in your URL bar. In Edge, type edge://extensions.

2

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the Developer Mode switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

3

Click "Load Unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left and select this ReelShield folder.

4

Enjoy Distraction-Free Browsing!

Visit YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook. Watch your reels counter bar track and protect your time.
