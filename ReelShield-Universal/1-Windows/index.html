What ReelShield Protects

Engineered specifically to solve infinite-scroll addiction across all major platforms.

YouTube Shorts & Pop-Ups

Completely removes Shorts shelves, sidebar tabs, in-player end screen pop-ups, and teaser cards. Automatically transforms /shorts/ URLs into focus overlays or standard desktop videos.

Instagram Reels Blocker

Hides the left navigation and bottom navigation Reels icons, removes suggested reels carousels from the feed, and intercepts direct navigation to /reels/.

Facebook Reels Blocker

Removes the "Reels and short videos" feed shelf, blocks navigation shortcuts, and redirects facebook.com/reel/* back to safe desktop browsing.

Circular Bordered Counter Pill

A non-intrusive floating rectangular bar with a circular bordered badge that displays your cross-platform count anywhere on screen. Isolated safely inside Shadow DOM.

How to Install ReelShield in Your Browser

ReelShield is built as a Manifest V3 extension compatible with Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Opera.

1

Open Extensions

Navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).

2

Enable Dev Mode

Turn on Developer mode in the top right corner.

3

Load Unpacked

Click Load unpacked and select the ReelShield directory.

4

Browse Focused

Visit YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook and see the blocker & counter in action!
